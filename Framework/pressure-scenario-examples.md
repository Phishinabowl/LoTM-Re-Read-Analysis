# Domain Pressure Examples And Historical Scenario Mapping

This library is referenced by the [testing methodology](testing_methodology.md), which owns policy.
CI 5.6 maps the former 17 independent named-scenario review entries into the existing semantic
pressure families. Original question wording below is preserved verbatim from `eafcb2a` for traceability.
The names are example references, not additional top-level review families or automatic passing evidence.

Every distinct structural question remains a required sub-obligation of its mapped semantic families.
Interpret named actors, works and settings as concrete examples of roles and relationships: equivalent
source-grounded or explicitly synthetic cases may substitute, provided they exercise all the same
distinctions and composed behavior. Do not require a growing replay of every franchise. Do not infer
that one selected example closes a family or that primitive conformance closes a composed question.
Exact facts about any selected real work/domain require its own verified evidence; this mapping
records requirements, not newly verified story facts, medical claims or legal conclusions.

## Domain Collections And Selection

The methodology's existing candidate catalog groups narrative/worldbuilding/media and non-narrative
domains (IT/operations, medical, legal/compliance, investigations, science/research). Those collections
are selection aids, not schema packs, new executable profiles or a second suite-membership catalog.
Cross-domain coverage still requires the domain diversity and ordinary/irregular/adversarial cases
specified in the methodology. New examples join a collection; only new durable semantic requirements
or deterministic defects justify additional permanent coverage.

## Review And Evidence Boundaries

- A family disposition must account for its original pressure matrix and every applicable semantic
  question in the mapped example rows. A blanket narrative/worldbuilding pass is insufficient.
- Shared fixtures may support several families. Query boundaries, composed interactions, source-grounded
  observations, ordinary failures and explicit missing capabilities remain independently accountable.
- No named example is silently reported as replayed. Record the actual selected sample and evidence mode.
- Release review admission remains pending; null evidence is not accepted proof. The current CI catalog
  does not implement acceptance of external review evidence, and consolidation does not enable it.
- No runtime fixture or executable case is retired by this organizational consolidation. Future removals
  require matched preconditions, expected behavior, failure modes and replacement evidence.
